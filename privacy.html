Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Telegram AI Social Publishing Agent is a service that helps its owner prepare and manage video content for social media publishing.

2. Information We Process

The service may process Telegram user identifiers, submitted video files, video URLs, generated captions, hashtags, and account authorization information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to receive video submissions, analyze content using AI services, prepare videos, and perform publishing actions authorized by the account owner.

4. Third-Party Services

The service may use Telegram, Google Gemini, TikTok and Instagram APIs to provide its functionality. Their respective privacy policies apply to their services.

5. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored in protected configuration files. Temporary video files may be deleted after processing. Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information.

6. Data Sharing

Video content and related information may be transmitted to third-party services when required to provide AI analysis or publishing functionality.
